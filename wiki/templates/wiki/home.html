About the Game

◇

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is a turn-based role-playing game developed by Sandfall Interactive. Set in a dark fantasy world inspired by Belle Époque France, the game follows the members of Expedition 33 on a desperate mission to destroy the Paintress.

Each year, the Paintress awakens and paints a number upon her monolith. Everyone of that age then disappears in an event known as the Gommage. With the number falling year after year, the members of Expedition 33 set out to end the cycle before their own time runs out.

Throughout their journey, the expedition encounters dangerous enemies, mysterious locations and powerful weapons while uncovering the secrets behind the Paintress and their world.

The game places a strong emphasis on exploration and storytelling as the party travels across the Continent. Players discover new areas, encounter unusual inhabitants and gradually uncover the history and mysteries of the world around them. The journey moves through a wide variety of environments, each contributing to the game's distinctive dark-fantasy atmosphere.

Alongside its story, Expedition 33 is heavily influenced by French art and culture, combining its Belle Époque inspiration with surreal environments and a painterly visual style. Its cinematic presentation, character-driven narrative and orchestral soundtrack help give the game its distinctive identity.

Reactive Turn-Based Combat

◇

Expedition 33 combines turn-based combat with real-time actions. Build your party, develop unique character builds and combine weapons, skills and abilities to create powerful strategies.

During combat, players can dodge, parry and counter enemy attacks in real time, while the free-aim system allows specific enemy weak points to be targeted for additional tactical opportunities.
