About the Game

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is a turn-based role-playing game developed by Sandfall Interactive. Set in a dark fantasy world inspired by Belle Époque France, the game follows the members of Expedition 33 on a desperate mission to destroy the Paintress.

Each year, the Paintress awakens and paints a number upon her monolith. Everyone of that age then disappears in an event known as the Gommage. With the number falling year after year, the members of Expedition 33 set out to end the cycle before their own time runs out.

Throughout their journey, the expedition encounters dangerous enemies, mysterious locations and powerful weapons while uncovering the secrets behind the Paintress and their world.
