Combat Styles

Each member of Expedition 33 approaches combat differently, with unique mechanics that encourage different strategies, combinations and playstyles in battle.

◇

Gustave

Overcharge

Gustave builds Charges for his Overcharge ability by using his other skills during battle. Once enough Charges have been accumulated, Overcharge can be unleashed to deal heavy damage and reward careful preparation throughout the fight.

◇

Maelle

Stances

Maelle moves between Offensive, Defensive and Virtuose Stances as she uses different abilities. Each stance changes the amount of damage she deals and receives, making stance management an important part of maximising her effectiveness in combat.

◇

Lune

Stains

Lune generates elemental Stains while using her abilities and can consume those Stains when casting other skills. Managing different combinations of Stains allows her to strengthen her abilities and makes planning the order of her attacks important.

◇

Sciel

Foretell

Sciel applies Foretell stacks to enemies before consuming them with certain abilities for additional effects. Her Sun and Moon skills also interact with her Twilight state, rewarding players who carefully build and consume Foretell during battle.

◇

Verso

Perfection

Verso's combat revolves around building his Perfection Rank, which progresses from lower ranks towards the powerful Rank S. Avoiding enemy attacks and performing well in combat allows him to maintain his momentum and increase the effectiveness of many of his abilities.

◇

Monoco

Bestial Wheel

Monoco can learn abilities from defeated Nevrons and use them as his own skills in battle. These abilities interact with his Bestial Wheel, which rotates between different Masks and provides bonuses depending on the active Mask.
